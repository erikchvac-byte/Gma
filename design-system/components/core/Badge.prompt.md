**Badge** — a small uppercase status or category pill.

```jsx
<Badge variant="urgent">Happy hour</Badge>
<Badge variant="discount">30% off</Badge>
<Badge variant="fresh">Live</Badge>
<Badge variant="stale">Stale</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral`, `fresh` / `stale` (auto leading dot), `urgent` (amber), `discount` (solid green).
