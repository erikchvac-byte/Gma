## 2026-07-13 — "Real price drops" value surface (Update, Story derivation-3-2)

- **Mode:** Update. Scope = ONE new in-app surface (the first consumer of derived value facts): a product-keyed "Real price drops" section over `price-vs-own-median`. - **Four layout rulings by Erik (AskUserQuestion, all recommendations accepted):**
  1. **Drop figure = both, side-by-side** — `19% below its usual` (percent = amber `{colors.discount}` mono headline) + `$32.40 vs $40.00 usual` (mono muted support). Mirrors the deal card's ADR-009 side-by-side honesty; the percent never floats free of its numbers.
  2. **Compact ledger rows in one section** (not per-drop mini-cards) — keeps the value band visually distinct from store deal cards.
  3. **Label + count header** — `Real price drops` (h2) + quiet mono count `4 today` (from `belowMedianCount`, honest/free).
  4. **Placement = between the distance slider and the store deal feed** — slider (unchanged) keeps its distance role over store deals; value section is product-keyed and not distance-filtered.
- **Honesty gates baked in:** Gate 2 (banner `discountPct` never appears on this surface — the whole point), ADR-009 (no verdict words — `below its usual` states the fact), ADR-066 (no health claims, facts-not-menus), Honest Math (suppress-not-fabricate; `pctVsMedian < 0` only; store-join miss / failed|stale store → row dropped).
- **Distinct empty posture:** unlike the deal feed's "No active deals right now", the additive value section renders **nothing** when there are no drops — silence, never a message that could imply the store feed is empty.
- **a11y:** own labeled `<section>` with an `<h2>`; rows are a SEPARATE local list, NOT nested in the feed's list (preserves the listitem-per-store count). Not a live region. Amber-on-white 5.4:1 AA per the DESIGN.md audit.
