Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Verdict (adversarial lens, WCAG 2.2 AA, ratios computed from frontmatter hex — not taken from the spec's own claims): The spine is well above industry baseline — the declared text pairs genuinely pass AA, dialog semantics are specified, and the calm-by-default live-region policy is defensible. But the spec self-contradicts on its strongest claim ("targets ≥ 44px for every interactive element" vs. a 22px slider thumb with no stated hit-target), leaves form-field boundaries at 1.47:1 against the 3:1 non-text floor on the primary cascade flow, and is silent on document-level basics (title, lang, landmarks, headings) plus several WCAG 2.2-specific criteria (2.5.7 dragging alternative, 2.4.11 focus-not-obscured under a sticky header). Nothing here forces a redesign; everything is fixable with token and wording changes.

What the spec gets right: every declared load-bearing text pair genuinely clears 4.5:1 under adversarial arithmetic; the focus ring clears 3:1 on every light surface with a specced white-gap fallback; the age-gate dialog contract is exactly right; countdown exclusion from aria-live [tagged ASSUMPTION] is the correct call; panel-scoped role="alert" for the fueleconomy failure is textbook error containment. Full computed contrast audit table in review-accessibility.md. The knife-edge urgent-badge finding is merged into Token completeness above.

Regulated content review

review-regulated-content.md

Verdict (regulatory / platform-risk lens — spec review, not legal advice): The spine's own voice is well-armored — no youth-coded language, no urgency theater, no consumption imagery, no invented savings claims — but the spec is silent exactly where an aggregator's regulatory surface actually lives: it republishes scraper-sourced retailer ad copy verbatim with no display constraint, and it ships zero standing disclaimers (no retailer-sets-the-deal line, no not-affiliated statement, no estimate qualifier on the gas figure, no WA-warning posture). These are spec gaps, not copy bugs, and two of them are launch-blocking-shaped.

Non-findings worth recording: "30% off"/happy-hour framing is factual third-party price information, not the aggregator's own promotion; brand is affirmatively adult-coded throughout; dispensary trade names containing slang ("Kush21 Everett") are factual licensed names, not product voice; age-gate persistence matches industry norm; consumption-depiction violations are structurally impossible (no imagery exists); web-only is a deliberate, documented sidestep of app-store cannabis policies; the countdown states the retailer's window without manufacturing pressure. Clean.

high

No rule constraining display of scraped deal descriptions

EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Card; Flow 1 step 3
The card renders "the description in {typography.body}"; Flow 1 step 3 renders "Top-shelf flower, every gram" verbatim; import data.js confirms descriptions are pass-through retailer copy. The app republishes third-party promotional text it does not control. WAC 314-55-155-family rules prohibit licensee advertising that appeals to minors, makes curative/therapeutic claims, or is false/misleading — a scraped description ("relieves pain", "kid-approved gummies", emoji, ALL-CAPS hype) would flow straight onto the page, simultaneously violating the brand's own no-slang/no-emoji rule and potentially importing a WAC violation onto Erik's surface.
Fix: Add a spec rule under Honest Math Rules or Component Patterns: "Deal descriptions are third-party retailer copy, displayed as plain text only: strip markup/emoji, cap length (~80 chars), and suppress (render the deal with badge + discount + window only) any description matching an operator-maintained blocklist of therapeutic-claim and youth-appeal terms. Descriptions are never rewritten or embellished client-side." Verify with counsel whether republishing retailer promotional copy makes the aggregator an "advertiser" under WAC 314-55-155.
high

Zero standing disclaimers anywhere in the IA

EXPERIENCE.md → Information Architecture; Voice and Tone
Four surfaces, no footer/legal surface; the Voice and Tone table has no disclaimer row. Three distinct lines are missing: (1) accuracy/affiliation — deals are set and honored by retailers, the site is not affiliated with any dispensary, and listings may be out of date despite "Last updated"; (2) the WA mandatory-warning family ("This product has intoxicating effects and may be habit forming…", "…may be purchased or possessed only by persons twenty-one years of age or older") — these attach to licensee advertising, and an aggregator's obligation is unsettled; (3) "informational only — nothing is sold here." The "Last updated" Notice is honest but is not a disclaimer.
Fix: Add a persistent feed-footer Notice (muted), e.g.: "Deals are set by each retailer and may change without notice — verify in store. Gma's Helper is not affiliated with any dispensary and sells nothing." Reserve a slot for WA warning text pending counsel. Verify with counsel which (if any) WAC warning statements apply to a non-commerce aggregator.
medium

Age gate has no decline path, by binding ruling — weakens good-faith posture under scrutiny

EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Age gate (ADR-021); Flow 3 steps 1–2
Self-attestation is the industry norm for non-commerce informational cannabis sites, and the specced copy is appropriately plain. But the prevailing norm is a two-option gate where decline exits or shows a terminal "you may not view this content" screen; a gate whose only affordance is affirmation reads as a formality if the gate is ever scrutinized. The decision-log ruling stands — this finding asks the spec to either add the decline affordance or record the defensibility rationale explicitly.
Fix: Prefer a secondary (ghost) "I am under 21" action leading to a static dead-end screen ("This site is for adults 21 and over."); failing that, add a spec sentence: "No decline path is a deliberate posture for a non-commerce informational site [ruled, ADR-021]." Verify with counsel before public launch.
medium

Gas figures present estimates as fact with no qualifier

EXPERIENCE.md → Honest Math Rules 2–3; DESIGN.md → Brand
"$1.46 to get there" reads "as measured fact," but the number is derived from a national-average or first-trim rated MPG, a regional EIA average price, and a road-distance lookup — it is an estimate, and the spec's own sheet copy already concedes this ("Estimated 32 MPG…") while the card does not. An unqualified dollar figure invites "your site said $1.46, it cost me $3" accuracy complaints and undercuts the honesty brand if ever challenged.
Fix: One global qualifier, not per-card clutter — extend the proposed footer disclaimer with: "Gas costs are estimates based on average fuel prices and rated MPG." Alternatively spec "est. $1.46 to get there" as the Discount Display string. Liability framing, not WAC; counsel optional.
