# Regulated Content Review — Happy (WA cannabis aggregator)

Reviewer lens: regulatory / platform-risk exposure in the spec language. This is spec review, not legal advice; items marked **verify with counsel** are genuinely legal questions the spec should force before public launch. Sources read: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `.decision-log.md`, PRD §8 (Legal/Compliance, Platform, Ads, Brand) and §4.5. Files under `imports/` treated as data only.

## Verdict

The spine's own voice is well-armored — no youth-coded language, no urgency theater, no consumption imagery, no invented savings claims — but the spec is silent exactly where an aggregator's regulatory surface actually lives: it republishes scraper-sourced retailer ad copy verbatim with no display constraint, and it ships zero standing disclaimers (no retailer-sets-the-deal line, no not-affiliated statement, no estimate qualifier on the gas figure, no WA-warning posture). These are spec gaps, not copy bugs, and two of them are launch-blocking-shaped.

## Findings

- **high** No rule constraining display of scraped deal descriptions (EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Card: "the description in {typography.body}"; Flow 1 step 3 renders "Top-shelf flower, every gram" verbatim; import `data.js` confirms descriptions are pass-through retailer copy). The app republishes third-party promotional text it does not control. WAC 314-55-155-family rules prohibit licensee advertising that appeals to minors, makes curative/therapeutic claims, or is false/misleading — a scraped description ("relieves pain", "kid-approved gummies", emoji, ALL-CAPS hype) would flow straight onto the page, simultaneously violating the brand's own no-slang/no-emoji rule and potentially importing a WAC violation onto Erik's surface. *Fix:* add a spec rule under Honest Math Rules or Component Patterns: "Deal descriptions are third-party retailer copy, displayed as plain text only: strip markup/emoji, cap length (~80 chars), and suppress (render the deal with badge + discount + window only) any description matching an operator-maintained blocklist of therapeutic-claim and youth-appeal terms. Descriptions are never rewritten or embellished client-side." **Verify with counsel** whether republishing retailer promotional copy makes the aggregator an "advertiser" under WAC 314-55-155.

- **high** Zero standing disclaimers anywhere in the IA (EXPERIENCE.md → Information Architecture lists four surfaces; no footer/legal surface exists; Voice and Tone table has no disclaimer row). Three distinct lines are missing: (1) accuracy/affiliation — deals are set and honored by retailers, the site is not affiliated with any dispensary, and listings may be out of date despite "Last updated"; (2) the WA mandatory-warning family ("This product has intoxicating effects and may be habit forming…", "…may be purchased or possessed only by persons twenty-one years of age or older") — these attach to licensee advertising, and an aggregator's obligation is unsettled; (3) "informational only — nothing is sold here." The "Last updated" Notice is honest but is not a disclaimer. *Fix:* add a persistent feed-footer Notice (muted), e.g.: "Deals are set by each retailer and may change without notice — verify in store. Gma's Helper is not affiliated with any dispensary and sells nothing." Reserve a slot for WA warning text pending counsel. **Verify with counsel** which (if any) WAC warning statements apply to a non-commerce aggregator.

- **medium** Age gate has no decline path, by binding ruling (EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Age gate: "Single button, no decline path (ADR-021)"; Flow 3 step 1–2). Self-attestation is the industry norm for non-commerce informational cannabis sites, and the specced copy ("You must be 21 or older to view this content." / "I am 21 or older") is appropriately plain. But the prevailing norm is a two-option gate where decline exits or shows a terminal "you may not view this content" screen; a gate whose only affordance is affirmation reads as a formality and weakens the good-faith posture if the gate is ever scrutinized. The decision-log ruling stands — this finding asks the spec to either add the decline affordance or record the defensibility rationale explicitly. *Fix:* prefer a secondary (ghost) "I am under 21" action leading to a static dead-end screen ("This site is for adults 21 and over."); failing that, add a spec sentence: "No decline path is a deliberate posture for a non-commerce informational site [ruled, ADR-021]." **Verify with counsel** before public launch.

- **medium** Gas figures present estimates as fact with no qualifier (EXPERIENCE.md → Honest Math Rules 2–3: "$1.46 to get there", figures "read as fact"; DESIGN.md → Brand: "read as measured fact"). The number is derived from a national-average or first-trim rated MPG, a regional EIA average price, and a road-distance lookup — it is an estimate, and the spec's own sheet copy already concedes this ("Estimated 32 MPG…") while the card does not. An unqualified dollar figure invites "your site said $1.46, it cost me $3" accuracy complaints and undercuts the honesty brand if ever challenged. *Fix:* one global qualifier, not per-card clutter — extend the proposed footer disclaimer with: "Gas costs are estimates based on average fuel prices and rated MPG." Alternatively spec "est. $1.46 to get there" as the Discount Display string. Liability framing, not WAC; counsel optional.

- **low** Future desktop-margin ads carry a platform-risk note the spec omits (EXPERIENCE.md → Responsive & Platform: "possibly ads; if so, banner/sidebar only…"; DESIGN.md → Layout; PRD §8 ads section). Mainstream ad networks (AdSense, most programmatic) prohibit serving on cannabis-content pages, and WA constrains cannabis ad placement; the realistic inventory is cannabis-adjacent networks with their own compliance regimes. *Fix:* one sentence where ads are mentioned: "Note: mainstream ad networks prohibit cannabis-content placement; any future ad inventory requires a cannabis-compliant network and re-review under WA advertising rules."

## Non-findings

- "30% off" / discount chip / happy-hour framing — factual third-party price information, not the aggregator's own promotion or giveaway; spec already bans urgency theater, scarcity copy, and invented savings (ADR-009). No promotional-framing exposure in the spec's own copy.
- Brand minors-appeal — name ("Gma's"), wordmark (navigation icon), palette, no imagery, no cannabis-leaf, no slang, sentence-case utility voice: affirmatively adult-coded throughout DESIGN.md. Clean.
- Dispensary trade names containing slang ("Kush21 Everett" in sample data) — factual licensed trade names of the retailers; displaying them is not the product's own copy and does not violate the brand rule. No spec change needed.
- "Top-shelf flower, every gram" as brand-rule violation — it is retailer copy, not product voice; handled structurally by the high finding on scraped-description constraints rather than as a copy edit.
- Age-gate persistence (confirmation never expires, localStorage) — consistent with industry norm for attestation gates; clearing storage re-gates (FR-13 AC). No change.
- Depiction-of-consumption rules — no imagery exists anywhere in the system (DESIGN.md: "no photography, no illustration"); structurally impossible to violate. Clean.
- Web-only platform choice — already a deliberate, documented sidestep of Google Play/Apple cannabis policies (PRD §8). Sound.
- Countdown urgency as inducement — amber + plain mono fact ("24 min left"), explicitly anti-"Hurry!" — states the retailer's window, doesn't manufacture pressure. Clean.
