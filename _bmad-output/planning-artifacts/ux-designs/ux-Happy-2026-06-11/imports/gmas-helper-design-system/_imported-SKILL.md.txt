## Fast orientation
- **What it is:** a single-purpose tool that answers "is this happy-hour deal worth the drive, right now?" — honest gas-cost-vs-savings math, a 21+ age gate, a distance slider. Budget-aware adults; **not** a stoner brand.
- **Foundations:** link `styles.css` (the only entry point). Tokens live in `tokens/`. Self-hosted fonts (Public Sans + IBM Plex Mono) in `assets/fonts/`; self-hosted Lucide icons + helpers in `assets/icons/` and `assets/icons.js`.
- **Components:** load `_ds_bundle.js`, then read primitives off `window.GmaSHelperDesignSystem_45dd11` (Button, IconButton, Badge, Card, RangeSlider, TextField, Select, Skeleton/SkeletonFeed, Notice). See each component's `.prompt.md`.
- **Full app recreation:** `ui_kits/app/` is a clickable reference build.

## Non-negotiables (read `readme.md` for the full rules)
- One action color: green-700. Amber = urgency/expiring only. Red = errors.
- Cards are a 1px gray-200 hairline on white — **flat, not shadowed**.
- Money / distance / time are always tabular IBM Plex Mono.
- Voice: say it straight. Sentence case. No hype, no cannabis slang, **no emoji**.
- 8px corners, 4px spacing grid, 44px hit targets, always-visible focus rings.
