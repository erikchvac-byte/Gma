# Decision Log — Gma's Helper Brief

## 2026-06-08

- **Working title chosen: "Gma's Helper"** (shifted from initial placeholder "Happy"). Fits the budget-conscious, older-skewing user profile — warm and unpretentious rather than cannabis-culture branded. Confirming with Erik whether this is the direction or still in flux.
- **Form factor: lightweight single-page web app**, not a native mobile app. Deliberate choice — Google Play bans cannabis-sale-facilitating apps outright (still enforced as of 2025-2026), and Texas's App Store Accountability Act (effective 2026-01-01) adds age-verification burdens to retail/cannabis apps. Web sidesteps both.
- **Deal sourcing: scrape/aggregate from publicly available dispensary websites and menus only.** No manual curation, no dispensary self-submission at launch — deliberate scope limit to keep this solo-feasible. Acknowledged risk: scraping carries ToS/legal and fragility (site-layout-change) considerations; flagged for downstream technical/architecture discussion.
- **Geography: 50 road-miles from zip 98270 (Marysville, WA), US only — this is the R&D starting point, not a permanent ceiling.** Expansion of the zone or relocation of the base point is open for later; v1 scope is fixed to this zone for validation purposes only. Must be road-distance (driving routes), not straight-line radius — explicitly excludes anything requiring a ferry crossing (no Olympic Peninsula, no Bremerton). This is a precise routing/distance-calc constraint that downstream technical work needs to honor exactly.
- **Three equal-priority user profiles**: (1) returning smoker unfamiliar with current pricing/shops, (2) retired budget-conscious regular buyer optimizing deal-vs-drive, (3) newcomer to area or to legal cannabis with zero local familiarity.
- **Six-month success bar, intentionally low: accuracy only.** Small test group (Erik, wife, maybe a friend) confirms displayed deals match real-world deals at the stores. No traffic or revenue targets at this stage — explicitly R&D/validation framing.
- **Monetization: non-intrusive ads only** (banner/sidebar, never pop-up/interstitial/blocking). Modest revenue goals, not aggressive. Ad aesthetic targets budget-aware adults who already know how to spot a deal — not youth- or cannabis-culture-branded.
- **Correction (2026-06-08): UX should be intuitive and easy, NOT "designed for the elderly."** Erik explicitly rejected an "older-audience aesthetic" framing for the product UX — the goal is clarity and low friction for everyone, not a patronizing "simplified for seniors" treatment. (The "older, budget-aware" framing applies only to ad-targeting style, not to the product's interface design.)
- **UX priority stated as a hard rule**: information-first, always — "if a design choice makes the page harder to use in exchange for ad placement, the ad loses." This is a durable product principle, captured in the brief itself.
- **Development approach guidance (process note, not brief content)**: Erik wants to start implementation with a coaching/guided path, and explicitly wants to be stopped and consulted if scope starts bloating or expanding silently. Carrying this forward as guidance for downstream PRD/architecture/dev-story work — not brief content, but should travel with the project.
- **Gas calculator detailed spec captured to addendum** (default = live national-average MPG refreshed every 24h, no hardcode; opt-in precision mode via fueleconomy.gov public API with Year/Make/Model cascading dropdowns behind a gear icon, persisted in browser). Brief itself describes this at the experience level only — addendum carries the implementation-shaping detail forward.
- **Working mode for this brief: Coaching path** — Erik chose to walk through section by section with pushback, given no urgent deadline.
