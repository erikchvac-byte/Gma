## Research Overview

1. Can I turn the data gma's collects into **new information** that Google's AI search will surface?

This report answers all three with current (mid-2026) sources. The short version up top, then the evidence.

### The one thing to understand first (it reframes everything)

The dispensaries in your screenshot are **not** there through Google Ads. They are there through **Google Business Profile** — the free local/Maps listings that require a **physical storefront** you can verify. That is a *third, separate door* from the two you were asking about. So there are really **three different doors**, and gma's eligibility is different at each:

| Door | What it is | Do the shops use it? | Can gma's use it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Google Business Profile** (the "Places" map pack in your screenshot) | Free local listings for businesses with a physical address | **Yes — this is what you saw** | **No** — requires a real storefront customers visit. gma's is a website, not a store. |
| **B. Google Ads** (paid) | Auction-based paid placements | Rarely — most can't | **Effectively no** for anything touching THC purchase intent (see §1) |
| **C. Organic + AI search** (free) | The blue links + AI Overviews / AI Mode | Yes, and it's their most durable channel | **Yes — and this is gma's real opening** (see §2) |

**Bottom line:** The channel you were pointing at (A) is closed to gma's by design, and the one you fear you need (B, paid ads) is mostly blocked for the whole vertical. The good news is that **Door C — AI/organic search — is exactly where gma's already-planned data strategy has a genuine, defensible edge.** That is where to invest.

## §1 — Google Ecosystem Eligibility: The Reality Check

### 1.1 Google Ads (paid) — mostly a dead end for this vertical **[High]**

- **THC/marijuana products: flatly prohibited** in the US. Google Ads policy bans the sale/promotion of psychoactive cannabis. ([Google Recreational-drugs policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16489299), [Google Dangerous-products policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6014299))
- **CBD: extremely narrow.** Only **topical, hemp-derived CBD ≤0.3% THC**, requires **LegitScript certification**, and can only target **California, Colorado, Puerto Rico**. Without LegitScript, "running Google Ads for any CBD product is effectively impossible — your ads will be disapproved and your account may be suspended." ([Puffboard 2026](https://puffboard.com/can-you-advertise-cbd-on-google-ads-in-2026/), [Sparks](https://sparkscann.com/google-ads-cannabis-compliant-advertising/))
- **The January 2026 policy update** turned out to be **only the extension of a Canada-only Search-ads pilot for licensed sellers** through Dec 31, 2026. It is Search-only, Canada-only, licensed-entity-only, and — importantly — **does not create any informational/directory carve-out**. ([Google Jan 2026 update](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16851502))
- **"Informational content is permitted"** in principle, but the moment your site's purpose is routing people to *buy* THC in a legal state, you sit in a grey zone where disapproval and account suspension are the norm. ([CannabisRegulations.ai](https://www.cannabisregulations.ai/cannabis-and-hemp-regulations-compliance-ai-blog/google-meta-tiktok-hemp-cbd-advertising-2026))

**Verdict:** Do **not** build the business on Google Ads. At most, a purely *educational* campaign ("Washington cannabis price data") might run, but it's fragile and not worth the account-suspension risk as a core channel. **[High]**

### 1.2 Google Business Profile — not available to a website **[High]**

GBP (the map pack you screenshotted) requires a **real business location or in-person service area**. A comparison/deal-finder website with no storefront and no in-person cannabis service **does not qualify**. This is the single biggest misread to correct: *you can't get gma's into that map pack because gma's isn't a place.* The shops earn it by being physical stores. **[High]**

> Nuance: cannabis-marketing guidance still says "optimize your Google Business Profile… AI Overviews lag in near-me searches" — but that advice is aimed at the *dispensaries*, not at aggregators. It's not a lever for gma's. ([mg Magazine](https://mgmagazine.com/business-strategy/marketing-advertising/ai-search-cannabis-seo/))

### 1.3 Google Shopping / Merchant — no **[High]**

Cannabis and CBD ingestibles are barred from Shopping/Merchant listings. Not applicable.

### 1.4 The honest summary of §1

Two of the three "Google ecosystem" doors are closed to gma's for structural reasons that have nothing to do with how good the product is. **Stop chasing paid ads and the map pack.** Everything below is about the door that *is* open.

## §2 — The Real Opportunity: AI + Organic Search (Door C)

### 2.1 How Google AI Overviews / AI Mode pick their sources **[High]**

- AI answers draw **~94–97% of citations from the organic top-20 pool** — so classic SEO still gates entry — but AI increasingly cites **lower-ranked pages with the clearest, best-structured claims**, not just #1. ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-impact-seo-2026))
- Selection factors: **entity authority** (consistent org identity, Organization schema), **E-E-A-T**, **backlink/domain trust**, and **content structured as clear, extractable claims**. ([eSEOspace](https://eseospace.com/blog/how-ai-overviews-impact-seo-2026/), [Google Search Central AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features))
- **Structured data helps understanding but is not a magic trigger** — AI Overviews use the same index as organic search, so "helpful, people-first content" is the real currency. ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features))

### 2.2 The cannabis-specific wall: YMYL suppression **[Medium–High]**

This is the catch, and you need to know it going in:

- Cannabis is a **"Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL)** topic. Google's AI (and ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini) apply an **extra safety gate** that **suppresses or heavily conservatizes** cannabis answers. A cannabis page can **rank on page 1 of normal search yet be entirely absent from the AI summary**. ([Lynx SEO](https://www.lynxseo.com/blog/hemp-brand-seo-ai-search-visibility/), [mg Magazine](https://mgmagazine.com/business-strategy/marketing-advertising/ai-search-cannabis-seo/))
- Combined with **~80% zero-click** on AI-triggered queries, this is a real headwind for the whole vertical. ([Lynx SEO](https://www.lynxseo.com/blog/hemp-brand-seo-ai-search-visibility/))

**But** — and this is the strategic core — the content types that **do** punch through the YMYL gate are exactly the ones gma's is uniquely positioned to produce:

| What AI cites *despite* YMYL suppression | Does gma's have it? |
|---|---|
| **Verifiable, first-party data** (lab reports, unique datasets, factual records) | **YES — this is gma's whole thing.** Derived price/deal/disparity data no one else publishes. |
| **Deep, substantive factual content** ("a 900-word FAQ beats a listicle") | Buildable from your data |
| **Strong entity/brand authority** (Organization schema, consistent identity, branded queries) | Buildable — partially specced in `spec_seo-crawler-visibility.md` |
| **Non-transactional, educational framing** | Your data reframed as facts, not a "buy here" funnel, dodges both the ad ban *and* some YMYL conservatism |

Source: [mg Magazine](https://mgmagazine.com/business-strategy/marketing-advertising/ai-search-cannabis-seo/), [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-impact-seo-2026).

### 2.3 Why gma's is unusually well-positioned here **[Medium]**

Your existing `project_ai-search-data-strategy.md` already had the right instinct: **turn commodity scraped data into proprietary relational facts.** This research confirms *why that is the winning move* — not just for SEO, but specifically because **unique first-party data is the one content class that survives the cannabis YMYL filter.** Examples of AI-citable "new information" gma's can mint from data it already holds:

- **"Cheapest delivered [product] near [town] this week"** — a computed fact (price + gas) that literally does not exist anywhere else.
- **"Average 1/8-oz price across Washington dispensaries, and how [store] compares"** — aggregate derived facts (you already compute disparities: 76 → 177).
- **"Which Washington dispensaries are running the deepest deals right now"** — a ranked, dated, verifiable list.
- **Trendlines** ("WA flower price is down X% this month") — the Phase B1 work already on your roadmap.

Each of these is a **citable claim with a number**, sourced to gma's, that a competitor's marketing-fluff page cannot match. That is what AI Overviews reward.

## §3 — Competitive Landscape

| Player | What they are | Model | Signal for gma's |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weedmaps** | Dispensary directory + deals finder | Pay-to-play: dispensary **subscriptions + featured listings + display ads**, $400–$1,500/mo (up to $10k+ in hot markets). ~$100–200M/yr revenue; historically ~$3,678/mo × 4,171 clients. ([MJBizDaily](https://mjbizdaily.com/cannabis-firm-weedmaps-aims-for-175-percent-revenue-growth/), [Miracuves](https://miracuves.com/blog/weedmaps-clone-revenue-model/)) | The proven money model = **stores pay for placement/leads.** Not display ads to consumers. |
| **Leafly** | Strain education + dispensary listings | Display ads + priority listings, $600–$4,000/mo. **Delisted from Nasdaq Jan 2025**, moved to OTC Pink. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leafly), [Sparks](https://sparkscann.com/weedmaps-vs-leafly-best-dispensary-platform/)) | Even the #2 brand struggled financially — this niche is real but **margin-hard**. Enter lean. |
| **dankreports / prices.dankreports.com** | State-level price *data tracker* | Data/authority play, state-sourced pricing | **Direct conceptual analog to gma's data angle** — but state-average, not store-level or delivered-cost. gma's is more granular. |
| **Statista, state DoH data** | Aggregate price stats | Reference/authority | These get cited by AI — proof that *cannabis price data as facts* is AI-surfaceable. |

**Positioning insight:** Weedmaps/Leafly are **directories that monetize placement**; the data-tracker sites are **authority plays with no clear revenue**. gma's differentiator — **honest, delivered (price+gas) cost and cross-store disparities at the store level** — sits between them and is **more AI-citable** than either. No incumbent owns "cheapest *delivered* deal near you." **[Medium]**

## §4 — Monetization Models, Ranked for gma's

Ranked by feasibility given cannabis constraints (ad bans, low cannabis CPMs, YMYL). **[Medium]** throughout — these are strategy judgments, not verified facts.

### Tier 1 — Best fit
1. **Dispensary referral / featured-placement fees (the Weedmaps model, scaled down).** Stores pay a flat monthly or per-lead fee to be featured/boosted in gma's feed and to claim/enhance their listing. This is the *proven* cannabis-aggregator revenue model and it **sidesteps Google entirely** — you're billing dispensaries directly, not running restricted ads. Start with a handful of WA stores you already scrape. **Highest-probability first dollar.**
2. **Data licensing.** You are accumulating a longitudinal, honest WA price/deal/disparity dataset (already 5,000+ observations, trendlines coming). Brands, dispensaries, and analysts pay for market intelligence. This is a *B2B* revenue line with no consumer-ad dependency and it compounds as your history deepens. Aligns perfectly with `project_dutchie-product-pricing.md`.

### Tier 2 — Viable, secondary
3. **Cannabis-friendly display ad networks** (not Google): **Mantis** is the leading cannabis/CBD network, self-serve, $250 min, 0% intermediary fee; **Traffic Roots** exists but pays notably lower CPMs. ([Mantis](https://www.mantisadnetwork.com/), [THC Affiliates](https://thcaffiliates.com/mantis-ad-network/)) Realistic but **cannabis CPMs are low** — this is pennies until you have real traffic. Bolt-on, not foundation.
4. **Affiliate (ancillary only).** ShareASale / GreenAffiliates carry cannabis-*ancillary* offers (grow gear, accessories, CBD). Small, tangential to your core deal-finder value. ([THC Affiliates](https://thcaffiliates.com/b2b/advertising-networks/))

### Tier 3 — Unlikely / avoid for now
5. **Consumer subscription.** Hard sell for a deals site; deals are inherently a free-user magnet.
6. **Google Ads / Shopping.** Blocked (see §1). Not a revenue path.

**Recommended revenue sequence:** Build audience via Door C (free AI/organic traffic) → monetize with **Tier 1 #1 (store placement fees)** once you have traffic to sell → layer **#2 data licensing** as the dataset matures → treat Mantis display as a small bolt-on. **[Medium]**

## §5 — Regulatory & Policy Constraints (keep these in view)

- **Google ad ecosystem:** THC ads banned US-wide; CBD ads near-impossible without LegitScript + narrow geo. Assume **zero** paid-Google support for the foreseeable future. **[High]**
- **YMYL AI suppression** applies to cannabis regardless of your compliance — design content to be **factual/educational/data-first**, not transactional, to maximize the chance of surviving the filter. **[Medium–High]**
- **WA state advertising rules (WSLCB):** Washington restricts cannabis advertising (placement, audience, appeal-to-minors rules). As an *information* site you have more latitude than a licensee, but any **paid promotion or store-paid placement** should be reviewed against WSLCB rules and age-gating expectations before you invoice a dispensary. **[Low — verify before monetizing]** *(Flagged for legal review, consistent with the Phase 0b note in `spec_seo-crawler-visibility.md`.)*
- **Age-gating + disclaimers via schema** are recommended both for compliance and as an AI trust signal. ([mg Magazine](https://mgmagazine.com/business-strategy/marketing-advertising/ai-search-cannabis-seo/))

## §6 — Prioritized Action Plan

Ordered by leverage. Items 1–4 are the ~80% and are within reach of work you've already specced.

**1. Mint derived "facts" pages from data you already hold (the AI wedge).** [High leverage]
   For each computed insight, publish a stable, crawlable, dated page stating the fact with the number and "as of" date: *cheapest delivered eighth near [town]*, *WA average price + who beats it*, *deepest deals this week*, *[store] vs WA median*. This is the direct execution of `project_ai-search-data-strategy.md` — and §2.2 explains *why* it's the one content class that beats YMYL suppression.

**2. Nail the entity/authority signals.** [High leverage]
   Organization schema, consistent "gma's / gmaslist.com" identity everywhere, an author/entity behind the data, license/disclaimer/age-gate schema. Much of this is already in `spec_seo-crawler-visibility.md` Phase 0a — ship that first; it's the ~90%-value blocker removal you already identified.

**3. Frame everything as data/education, not "buy weed here."** [Medium leverage]
   This simultaneously (a) keeps you clear of the Google ad ban's transactional trigger and (b) improves odds against the YMYL filter. Link out to stores as *references/citations*, not as a checkout funnel.

**4. Instrument AI-citation tracking.** [Medium leverage]
   Track when gma's data appears in AI Overviews / is cited by Perplexity/ChatGPT, the way you'd track backlinks. Treat a missing citation as a content gap to fix. ([mg Magazine](https://mgmagazine.com/business-strategy/marketing-advertising/ai-search-cannabis-seo/))

**5. Stand up the first revenue line: store featured-placement.** [Money]
   Once pages from #1 pull traffic, offer 3–5 WA dispensaries a low flat monthly to be featured. Validate willingness-to-pay before building billing infrastructure. **Verify WSLCB ad rules first (§5).**

**6. Package the dataset for licensing.** [Money, compounding]
   As trendlines accrue (Phase B1), assemble a simple "WA cannabis price intelligence" report/API and test B2B demand with brands/dispensaries.

**7. Do NOT invest in:** Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Google Shopping. Structurally closed (§1). Revisit only if US federal cannabis rescheduling materially changes Google's policy — watch for it, don't plan on it.
